# Agent icon assets

The OpenAI Blossom and Claude starburst identify their respective agents. The
monochrome and orange variants come from Lobe Icons static SVG 1.94.0 (MIT).
The other brand marks use that package or Herdr's shipped icons. See the
installed Herdr icons/LICENSES.md for attribution to the shipped sources.
All brand marks belong to their owners.

Sources:
- https://openai.com/brand/
- https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons
- https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr-app

The Crush, Pig, and auxiliary selectable glyphs were drawn for this layout.
